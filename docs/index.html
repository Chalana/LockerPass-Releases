Everything you need to sign in safely

Stop remembering and retyping passwords. Use a strong, different one for every site, and let LockerPass do the work.

Fill logins in a click

Pick a login from the menu in the sign-in field, in your browser or in apps on your phone. LockerPass suggests a strong password when you sign up, and offers to save new logins and update changed passwords.

Two-factor codes

Scan a website's setup QR code and LockerPass shows the six-digit code and fills it for you, so you don't need a separate authenticator app.

Passkeys

Create and use passkeys on websites that support them. They're kept in your vault and sync like everything else.

Watchtower

Find weak, reused and leaked passwords, duplicate logins to merge, and sites where you could turn on two-factor sign-in. Get a reminder before a card or document expires.

Cards, addresses and more

Fill cards and addresses at checkout. Keep secure notes, IDs, bank accounts, Wi-Fi passwords, software licences and SSH keys too.

Unlock with Windows Hello or a fingerprint

Unlock with your master password once, then with your face, fingerprint or PIN. LockerPass locks itself when you step away.

Sync and work offline

Your vault is on your computer and phone, and works without a connection. Changes sync, encrypted, when you're back online.

Bring your passwords

Import from Chrome, Edge, Brave, Firefox, 1Password, Bitwarden and LastPass. Export whenever you like: your data is yours.

Private by design

LockerPass is built so that your passwords can't be read by anyone but you, even if our servers were broken into.

Encrypted on your device

Your vault is encrypted on your computer or phone before anything is synced. Only encrypted data ever reaches the cloud.

Two secrets, only yours

Unlocking needs your master password and your Secret Key, a 128-bit key made on your device. Neither is ever sent to us.

No tracking

No ads, no analytics, no selling data. We don't know what's in your vault or which sites you use.

Travel mode

Crossing a border? Turn on travel mode and only the vaults you've marked safe for travel stay on your devices. Turn it off and the rest come back.

Keep your Emergency Kit safe

When you create your account, LockerPass gives you an Emergency Kit with your Secret Key and Recovery Code. Print it or save it somewhere safe. If you forget your master password, the Recovery Code is the only way back into your vault: we can't reset it for you, because we never have the keys.

Setting up a phone

Install LockerPass on your phone and sign in. On your computer, open Settings → Devices → Set up a phone, and scan the code with your phone. Then enter your master password.
